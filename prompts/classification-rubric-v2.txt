CLASSIFICATION RUBRIC — VERSION 2

Apply this decision order. Classify the demonstrated condition, not the wording
of a recommendation.

1. major_nc: choose only when evidence explicitly demonstrates that a required
   management-system process or essential planning element is absent, not
   established, or systemically not applied.
2. minor_nc: choose only when evidence explicitly demonstrates failure of a
   specific requirement, while the wider process still exists and no systemic
   failure is demonstrated.
3. ofi: choose when the required outcome appears available or functioning and
   the evidence supports a concrete improvement in efficiency or practice,
   without demonstrating a requirement failure.
4. observation: choose when evidence raises a concern but leaves the required
   outcome unverified. Recommendations, incomplete inventories, missing
   preferred formats, or phrases such as "needs review" do not by themselves
   demonstrate nonconformity.

Boundary examples:
- A policy exists and notes merely say it needs review, without identifying
  deficient required content or failed communication: observation.
- A list of roles is incomplete, without evidence that required roles are
  unassigned or uncommunicated: observation.
- A formal training course is absent, but awareness or competence itself has
  not been shown absent: observation.
- Required documents are available but retrieval is slow: ofi.
- Audit capability should be strengthened, while missed required audits or an
  absent audit programme have not been demonstrated: observation.
- A required documented scope exists conceptually but is explicitly not
  documented: minor_nc.
- A required risk process is explicitly not in place: major_nc.

When facts support more than one reading, choose observation and set
needs_human_review to true. Do not upgrade severity to compensate for
uncertainty.
